# The Lantern

**The question:** will her brother ever answer?
**The answer:** yes. He saw every lantern she sent.

No narration. The words are on screen.

1. Every autumn, she sent a lantern to her lost brother.
2. Ten autumns. No answer.
3. Then the wind turned, and one came back.
4. On it, in his writing: I saw them all.

**Logic.** A sky lantern rises on the heat of its flame and goes where the wind goes. Hers leaves on a wind blowing left. His arrives only when the wind turns and blows from the left, and it sinks to her because its flame is low.

**Sound.** Quiet music and wind. The wind swells as it turns, and paper sounds when she lets go and when she catches. A soft bell when the words are clear. The music lifts from sad to hopeful at the turn.
