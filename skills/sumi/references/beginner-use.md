# Make your first film, one step at a time

You do not need to know animation or editing. Your assistant works as a small studio, explains each decision in plain language and guides you through every step of every film. Start after [installation](install/shared.md). If you get lost at any point, just say: "I don't understand, explain it more simply."

## 1. Start Beyondsumi

Copy this into a fresh chat:

> Use the sumi skill. I am a beginner. Read its SKILL.md, check what this computer and assistant can do, and guide me one step at a time. Do not generate a film, buy API usage or download models during setup.

**You should see:** a short welcome and a clear list of what is available or missing. If you get only a generic answer, give the assistant the exact path to `sumi/SKILL.md` and ask it to read that file.

## 2. Choose art, voice and sound

- **Art:** Sumi Dark is black and white brush, always with vivid red and one more vibrant colour, for horror, mystery and adventure. Sumi Light is clear, dark colour pencil sketch, for children's stories and love stories. Sumi Vector is clean lines on painted skies, drawn in code with no images, for any story. You can ask for one image comparing the styles.
- **Pictures:** ChatGPT and Codex use the best image model they have. Claude asks you for access to a third-party image tool, or animates in code with Sumi Vector. You can also supply your own art.
- **Voice:** Voicebox, free on your computer after a model download you approve; ElevenLabs, paid; your own recording; or no narration. Story language uses your assistant, and spoken languages depend on the voice model.
- **Sound:** every film has background music and sound effects, with or without narration.

The assistant explains any missing item before installing or paying for anything. You do not have to connect every option.

## 3. Give your idea, story or recording

For example:

> Help me write a 30-second story about a lonely puppy finding a friend. I want warm narration. Show me the story before making anything.

If something is missing, the assistant asks how long the film should be, then whether you want a narrator, character dialogue or both. It helps with the hook, the feeling and an ending that gives a clear answer. You can also give a finished story and ask it to keep your words, or send your recorded narration.

## 4. Review the script and the plan

Read the full words first, and ask for changes in plain language. Then the Director shows:

- the recommended style and why
- each scene in plain words
- the estimated length
- usual movement, or smoother movement with more real drawings, which takes more images
- how many images it will take, and the cost
- the character voices and the sound, and lip-sync for characters seen speaking, which is recommended and adds a few drawings

If the whole story needs longer than you asked for, choose the full version or a shorter version it offers. Ask for changes here, because changes cost nothing yet. Nothing is made until you approve this plan.

## 5. Approve and review the proof

Say: "Approve this script and plan; create the proof." Only a paid image, voice or download you approved may run.

If your film has speech, the voice is made and approved first, because the drawings and mouths follow it. If that changes the number of images or the cost, the assistant asks you again.

The Artist shows one sample and your characters. The Animator shows the main poses, then the hardest shot moving, before the whole film is made. Tell it what feels wrong, such as missing emotion, a confusing action, weak sound or a wrong hand, or "the turn is too fast". It should look into the problem before replacing more art.

## 6. Receive and use your film

Before you see it, the Checker tests that the animation is real, and five test viewers and one inspector check the film. They tell you anything that is still not perfect.

You receive an HD MP4, your project files and your own Beyondsumi dashboard to watch and download the film. Watch the real video with sound, because the assistant cannot hear it, then use Download. A local dashboard link works while its local server runs. The dashboard shows all three styles, and "Make this edition" prepares a request for your assistant. It never secretly makes another film.

For changes, say what and where, such as "at the gate, the drums are too quiet before they stop". The assistant reuses what is good and fixes only what is affected. Your original recording and earlier film are kept. Captions stay visible even with no narration, unless you ask for a film with no text.
