# Build around the user's recorded narration

Offer three narration choices in simple words: **Voicebox**, **ElevenLabs**, **record your own voice and send it**. Keep “skip narration—music and effects only” as a separate easy action. Narration can help follow a story, but it is optional.

For a supplied recording, confirm whether it is the **full spoken story** or a **voice sample**. Use a full story directly; do not clone it or send it to a speech provider by default. Preserve the original bytes, measure duration/sample rate and check playback. Understand the actual words, language, pauses, tone and emotional turns using the host's real audio/transcription capability. If unavailable, explain that limit and use supplied text or an explicitly approved transcription route; do not pretend to hear the recording. Voicebox ASR may need another model download, requiring consent. Do not infer or claim personal traits from a voice.

Transcribe/reconcile the spoken content, identify unclear words and ask only necessary questions. Develop story beats, scene direction, imagery, camera and captions around the actual narration/performance. If the user has only a rough idea, help write first; if the recording contains the complete story, keep the meaning/wording. Ask before adding/removing speech, rushing/retiming it or changing the duration. For>30 seconds retain the full estimate versus shorter-alternative approval contract. Do not manufacture pauses to hit a nominal runtime.

Choose a visual style with the user. Then plan visible actions and emotional/environmental responses at actual spoken cues, preserving continuous pacing, complete character geometry and story consequences. Add library music and effects as in [Sound](../../sumi-sound/SKILL.md). Music and SFX support speech, with contact-aligned cues and duck/recovery; do not bury the recorded voice. Keep source/narration, music, ambience and effects as separate stems. Noise reduction/leveling should preserve the performance; keep an untouched source and check actual listening.

Recommend a clear quiet recording with consistent distance/level, no music or overlapping speakers. Their own voice can make the channel recognizable; it does not guarantee monetization. A licensed preset/consented distinctive voice is also fine. Ask whether to save only the chosen route for later; private recordings, transcripts and credentials stay outside the generic ZIP.
