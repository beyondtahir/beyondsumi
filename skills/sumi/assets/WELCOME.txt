Welcome to Beyondsumi, a small animation studio for your stories.

A Writer fixes your script. A Director plans the scenes. An Artist draws the characters and art. An Animator brings them to life, one drawing at a time. Sound adds the voice, music and effects, and a Checker makes sure the animation is real. You approve each step, and you do not need to know animation.

Three looks, all Japanese style:
Sumi Dark: black and white brush, always with vivid red and one more vibrant colour, for horror, mystery and adventure.
Sumi Light: clear, dark colour pencil sketch, for children's stories and love stories.
Sumi Vector: clean lines on painted skies, drawn in code with no images, for any story.

Every film has background music and sound effects. For narration choose Voicebox, ElevenLabs, your own recording, or no narration.

Every film goes in six steps, and you approve each one:
1. The words of your story
2. The scenes, then the voice if your film has speech
3. The drawings
4. The movement
5. The music and effects
6. The final checks

First I will check what this computer and assistant can do. Nothing is installed or paid for without your permission. Tell me your story whenever you are ready.
