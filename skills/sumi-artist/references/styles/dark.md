# Sumi Dark

Black and white brush painting with small vibrant yellow and red. For horror, mystery and adventure.

## The look

- **Brush:** heavy, expressive wet strokes that swell from thick to thin, with dry, broken edges. The hand of the painter is visible and decisive.
- **Tone:** pure black, grey washes and white paper, with strong contrast. Far things dissolve into grey wash and bare paper.
- **Empty space:** white or black emptiness is part of the picture. It is where fear lives.
- **Colour:** only small, vibrant accents of yellow and red, such as a flame, a sash or a drop of blood. If a frame has more than two small accents, it has too much colour.
- **Marks:** ink splashes and paper grain.
- **Characters:** Japanese anime faces drawn with very few strokes.

**Always use the accent.** Every Sumi Dark film uses vivid brush red, plus one more vibrant colour such as yellow, in the drawings themselves: a flame, a sash, a flower, a spatter, blood. Put at least one accent in most shots. A film that is only black, white and grey has missed the style.

## Prompt base

> Bold Japanese sumi-e ink painting in black and white on white washi paper. Heavy expressive wet brush strokes, strong contrast, dry-brush texture, ink splashes, grey washes dissolving into empty paper. Japanese anime character, face drawn with very few strokes. The only colours are small vibrant accents of bright yellow and vivid red. Everything else is pure black, grey and white. No pencil lines, no text, no seal.

## Separate layers

- **Character:** on plain white paper, with no background, so it cuts out cleanly.
- **Background:** its own painting, with no people, wider and taller than the screen so it can scale and move.
- **Foreground:** a door frame, branch or pillar on plain white paper, kept dark and bold.

## Failure list

- Grey and muddy, with no real black.
- Soft digital gradients instead of brush.
- Colour spreading beyond small accents.
- Fine anime shading, or pencil lines.
- Busy backgrounds with no empty space.
