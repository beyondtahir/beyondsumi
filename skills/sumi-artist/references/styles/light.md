# Sumi Light

A clear, dark colour pencil sketch. For children's stories and love stories.

## The look

- **Line:** crisp, confident, dark pencil outlines with firm pressure. The drawing must be easy to read, never faint.
- **Shading:** deep colour built from dense, visible pencil hatching, with strong shadows. Darker shades, not pale pastel.
- **Colour:** rich and warm: indigo, deep vermilion, dark green, charcoal.
- **Paper:** warm white paper, with some of it left bare. Visible strokes show it was drawn by hand.
- **Characters:** Japanese anime faces and proportions, drawn like a key animator's sketch.

## Prompt base

> Japanese anime character drawing in coloured pencil on warm white paper. Crisp, confident, clearly readable dark pencil outlines with firm pressure, deep rich shading from dense coloured pencil hatching, strong shadows, rich warm colours. Clearly hand-drawn with visible pencil strokes and some bare paper. Not washed out, not faint, not a digital painting, no ink wash, no text.

## Separate layers

- **Character:** on plain paper, so it cuts out cleanly.
- **Background:** drawn as frame sheets, because it animates. Keep it simpler and a little lighter than the character, so the character stands out and stays consistent.
- **Scene changes:** drawn on transition sheets: lines drawing themselves, a scribble, an eraser or a page turn.

## Failure list

- Faint or washed-out pencil.
- Smooth digital colour with no strokes.
- Ink wash or brush texture, which belong to Sumi Dark.
- European storybook faces.
- Backgrounds so detailed that they flicker from frame to frame.
