# Directing each style

## Sumi Dark: horror, mystery, adventure

The most cinematic style. Dread comes from what is hidden, so use black and empty space to hold things back.

**Camera**
- A slow push toward a character as fear grows, then a sudden stop.
- A tilted frame when the world feels wrong.
- A fast push to the eyes for a realisation.
- A short shake only on real impact.
- Hold completely still before something bad happens. Stillness is the wind-up.

**Parallax, from separate images**
- The character is cut out on their own layer.
- The background is its own painting that scales or moves behind them, or changes to a different background.
- A foreground object, such as a door frame or branch, passes close to the camera.
- Typical depths: far background 0.2 to 0.4, character 1.0, foreground 1.5 to 2.

**Effects people use**
- **Atmosphere:** drifting smoke and fog, rain, embers, flickering light on a face.
- **Ink:** splashes on impact, ink spreading across paper, ink bleeding in red.
- **Reveals:** silhouettes, a face coming out of black, a shape behind the character before they see it.

Effects are drawn frame by frame on their own layer. Keep red and the one vibrant colour to small touches, so that they mean something, but never leave them out.

**Scene changes:** drawn ink splash or ink wipe, or a hard cut for shock.

## Sumi Light: children's stories and love stories

Warm, clear and gentle. It should feel like a sketchbook come alive.

**Camera:** cinematic but soft. Gentle pushes, slow pans across the page and quiet reframing. No shaking or tilting.

**Movement**
- The character and the background are both animated with drawn frames. Trees, water, clouds and passers-by move as drawings.
- **No parallax.** All layers share one depth, so the page moves as one.

**Scene changes, in sketch**
- Pencil lines draw the next scene, then colour fills in.
- A scribble covers the screen and clears to the next shot.
- An eraser wipes the old scene away.
- A sketchbook page turns.

These are drawn on a transition sheet, not made with code.

## Sumi Vector: any story

Clean, cinematic Japanese TV animation of the 1990s and 2000s.

**Camera**
- Slow pans across long painted backgrounds.
- Push-ins for emotion, and matched cuts between angles.
- Held dramatic frames with small movements, like hair in the wind.

**Movement:** clean drawn acting at 12 drawings a second, and 24 for big action. **No parallax.**

**Scene changes:** clean cuts and dissolves, with no ink or sketch effects.

## Example: one shot in Sumi Dark

A girl hears something behind her.

- **Purpose:** the audience should feel fear before she does.
- **Framing:** wide, with her small on the right and black empty space on the left.
- **Camera:** a slow push in, then a sudden stop when she freezes.
- **Layers:** far wall (depth 0.3), girl (1.0), door passing close to the camera (1.8).
- **Acting:** breath, stillness, then the head slowly turns, with the eyes moving first.
- **Effect:** smoke drifting, and the yellow lamp flickers once.
- **Sound:** the room goes silent just before the turn.
- **Drawings:** 3 seconds at 12 per second is 36. The turn gets 24 per second. The stillness uses a small breathing cycle.
