# Dialogue and casting

Before any voice work, separate the narrator's words from the words a character speaks. Record each speaking role, the exact approved words, the language, the feeling and the source in the project's `VOICE-MAP.json`, copied from `assets/templates/VOICE-MAP.json` in the sumi skill.

- **Cast the character.** Give direct speech to the character's voice, and do not repeat it in the narrator's voice. Generate each line as its own take, and show the cost first.
- **Use only what the voice tool really supports.** Do not send tags to a model that would read them aloud. Voicebox's voices, cloning, languages and delivery controls differ by engine and version, so offer only what is installed, and get consent before a model download or using anyone's voice.
- **Recordings:** match each file to its speaker and exact line, or use time ranges in one combined file. Ask only about what is unclear. Never replace a recording with a generated voice, clone it, change its feeling, cut speech or stretch it without permission. Keep the original.
- **Children:** say who is really performing, and include it in what the user approves. Offer the user's own recording, or a young, bright voice. Never promise a child voice, and do not get round a voice library's rules. ElevenLabs does not allow child or child-like voices in its public Voice Library.
- **Lip-sync:** when a character is seen speaking, follow [lip-sync](../../sumi-animator/references/lip-sync.md).

Check the finished film at each line's start, pauses, end and the cuts beside it: the right speaker, mouth timing, feeling, complete words and the balance with music and effects.
