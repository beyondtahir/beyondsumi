# Welcome to Beyondsumi

You do not need to know animation, editing or code. [The install guide](INSTALL.md) has one message to paste into your assistant, and it does the rest. After that, your assistant guides you through every film, one step at a time. You can also read [make your first film](skills/sumi/references/beginner-use.md).

Bring an idea, a written story or your recorded narration. Ask your assistant to use `skills/sumi/SKILL.md`. It runs the studio in order: writer, director, artist, animator, sound and checker. You review the script and the plan before anything is made, and only the style you choose is made.

The optional installer copies every skill to your assistant's skills folder:

```
python3 install.py --skills-root /absolute/path/to/your/host/skills
```

It checks what is on your computer and installs no other software or models. You also need Node 22+, Chrome, Edge or Chromium, and FFmpeg. Paid images and voices need your approval first. Never paste keys into chat.
